# Product vision

Stand: 2026-07-28

## Product statement

Aquata is a seasonal, asynchronous multiplayer strategy game about rising from a vulnerable underwater station to a major power through growth, reconnaissance, deception, coordinated fleet operations, and community.

Each season starts with scarcity and limited options, escalates toward large political and military conflicts, reaches a deliberate climax, and then resets competitive power. Relationships, reputation, memories, and identity continue across seasons.

## Player fantasy

The player is not an administrator filling resource forms. The player is a commander who:

- builds an increasingly capable station and fleet,
- reads an uncertain strategic situation,
- identifies worthwhile targets,
- distinguishes opportunity from traps,
- coordinates allies across distance and time,
- conceals intent and creates believable feints,
- survives costly setbacks,
- participates in a political history created by the players.

## Desired feelings

Aquata should repeatedly create:

- pride in growing from almost nothing,
- anticipation while an operation is underway,
- tension caused by incomplete information,
- satisfaction from a well-chosen target,
- relief when a defense or bluff succeeds,
- trust and shared ownership inside a group,
- memorable victories, losses, rivalries, and reversals,
- renewed possibility when a new season begins.

## Product pillars

### Seasonal rise

The contrast between early scarcity and late-game scale is essential. Small early fleets, slow first journeys, and hard economic choices make later power meaningful.

### Strategic uncertainty

Targets are not simple power comparisons. Information has age, precision, provenance, cost, and counterplay. Geography, possible reinforcements, scan defense, and deception matter.

### Strategic world

The generated map is a primary game system rather than a station directory. Uneven resource geography creates consequential placement, dense and quiet neighborhoods, local opportunity, and room for new points of interest across later seasons. The first version should prove this foundation with few complete world interactions instead of filling the map with shallow content.

### Coordinated operations

The deepest play comes from players acting together: supporting attacks, defending allies, pressuring several targets, withholding reserves, and launching decoys.

### Community as gameplay

Conversation, trust, reputation, agreements, and betrayal affect strategic possibilities. Communication is not an auxiliary feature; it is part of the game system.

### Fair but consequential conflict

Losses must matter, but a single defeat should not normally end a player's season. Protection, recovery, salvage, and transparent attack boundaries must preserve both tension and the possibility of a comeback.

### Compatible with normal life

Aquata must support a satisfying and strategically meaningful game while sleeping normally. Competitive mastery should come primarily from planning, judgment, coordination, and prioritization.

Players who voluntarily check more often, cover unusual hours, or organize a night watch may gain a slight to moderate advantage through fresher information and additional reaction opportunities. That edge may matter at the top of the competition, but it must not make normal play futile or turn trivial alarm-clock clicks into the main path to power.

Aquata does not create a global night or make every station disappear into routine nightly protection. Healthy sleep comes from long minimum warning times, alliance members with complementary schedules, bounded losses, and recovery. A late response may save a fleet without preventing economic damage; remaining continuously awake should not be necessary to preserve a viable season.

## What resets and what persists

Competitive seasonal power resets:

- resources,
- collectors and production capacity,
- ships and fleets,
- station upgrades,
- research and seasonal unlocks,
- seasonal achievement progress and its gameplay rewards,
- map position and territorial influence,
- season-specific alliance standings, holdings, and objectives.

Persistent identity and community history survive the reset without granting decisive starting power:

- canonical account, login, roles, and moderation history,
- public profile, chosen imagery, and social graph,
- reputation and long-term statistics,
- friendship and rivalry history,
- alliance lineage and past treaties,
- hall-of-fame entries,
- selected battle reports and season stories,
- cosmetics, titles, and prestige,
- cross-season or meta achievements that provide recognition without competitive power,
- community contributions such as persistent discussions or guides.

A player may present a themed or incognito identity during a season without creating a second canonical account. This public season identity never hides the player from operators or creates an additional seasonal player. The complete model is defined in IDENTITY_SAFETY_AND_ACCOUNT_LIFECYCLE.md.

## Explicit non-goals

Aquata should not become:

- a direct recreation of the old PHP browsergame,
- an idle resource counter with combat attached,
- a collection of long administrative forms,
- a game won mainly through alarm-clock activity,
- a permanent world in which established leaders cannot be caught,
- a pay-to-win economy that sells military power or removes strategic timing,
- a generic chat app disconnected from game context,
- a feature-complete legacy port before the modern core loop is proven.

## Product posture

Preserve the emotional and strategic DNA, not every historical rule. Mechanics from the old game are candidates only when they strengthen this vision in a form that is legible, fair, and enjoyable today.
